What I can help with

Everyday home projects, handled professionally.

From mounting and assembly to repairs and fixture replacements, Dr. Handyman Service LLC helps homeowners take care of the jobs that keep a home working and looking its best.

TV Mounting

Secure TV mounting, including large TVs, fireplace installations and wire-management options.

Furniture Assembly

Beds, dressers, desks, TV stands, tables, cabinets and other ready-to-assemble furniture.

Drywall & Wall Repair

Patches, holes, minor wall damage, touch-ups and clean finishing for common household repairs.

Doors, Locks & Hardware

Door adjustments, lock and smart-lock installation, handles, latches, lockboxes and hardware replacement.

Faucets & Toilet Repairs

Faucet replacement, toilet components, wax rings, caulking and other minor plumbing fixture work.

Fans & Light Fixtures

Ceiling fan and light fixture replacement where existing connections are suitable for installation.

Shelves, Mirrors & Curtains

Professional mounting of shelves, mirrors, artwork, curtain rods, blinds and wall accessories.

Home Installations

Microwaves, range hoods, bathroom accessories, cabinet hardware and many common household installations.

General Handyman Repairs

Small repairs and punch-list items. Send photos and a description for a quick project review.
